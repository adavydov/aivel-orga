# Page topology

1. **Sticky header**
   - Brand: `Aivel / роль`
   - Anchors: Context, Focus, Functions, Organization, Boundaries, Metrics

2. **Hero**
   - Eyebrow: candidate pre-read
   - Governing answer
   - Four contract metrics
   - Two anchors: see mandate / see boundaries

3. **Context**
   - Why the role exists now
   - Three business motions shown as a structural row

4. **Focus**
   - Ordered priority: SMB → Enterprise → accounting outsourcing
   - One concrete outcome per priority

5. **Functional map**
   - Three segment demand engines
   - Shared Product Marketing function
   - Shared Brand function

6. **Target organization**
   - Key role cells the Marketing Director must assemble
   - Segment owners plus shared capabilities
   - Role cells are mandatory; temporary combination is allowed

7. **Boundaries**
   - Separate blocks for Product, Commerce, and M&A
   - Each block names marketing ownership, counterpart ownership, and shared seam

8. **Metrics**
   - Common outputs: owned base, target inquiries, held meetings
   - SMB: contact-base size and the ability to price above market through brand strength
   - Enterprise: target pipeline and revenue
   - Accounting outsourcing: target-company revenue and acquisition multiple

9. **Closing**
   - Restates the mandate in one sentence
   - Uses the next meeting to resolve differences

10. **Footer**
    - Aivel
    - Purpose and date
