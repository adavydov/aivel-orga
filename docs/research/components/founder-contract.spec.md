# CEO contract specification

## Overview

- Target: `src/components/FounderContractSection.tsx`
- Interaction: static

## Structure

- indexed section
- lead
- two-column before/after-style contract panel
- joint-decisions row

## CEO commitments

- keep the priority order explicit
- set the strategic and resource frame
- give access to product, experts, cases, and approved deal positions
- make final calls where product promise, public claim, or M&A terms change company risk

## Director commitments

- build the functions and operating system
- choose channels and execution methods inside the agreed frame
- keep Product Marketing separate from Digital
- deliver the agreed base, inquiry, meeting, revenue, and multiplier outcomes

## Joint decisions

- product offer and launch promise
- resource allocation between the three priorities
- claims that affect enterprise delivery or M&A economics

## Exact styles

- two columns divided by an accent arrow
- accent labels, large sans headings, muted body
- joint row under the panel with three equal cells
- mobile stacks and turns arrow horizontal
